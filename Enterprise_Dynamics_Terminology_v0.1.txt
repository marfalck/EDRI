------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

An enterprise is an organised system of capabilities assembled to achieve specific objectives within a continuously changing operating landscape.

Purpose

The enterprise is the primary system studied by Enterprise Dynamics.

It is viewed as a dynamic system rather than an organisational chart.

What it is not

- A legal entity
- An organisation chart
- A collection of departments

Examples

- Startup
- SME
- Multinational corporation
- Government agency
- Charity
- Military organisation

------------------------------------------------------------
Operating Landscape
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The continuously evolving external environment within which an enterprise operates.

Purpose

Defines the context to which the enterprise must continuously adapt.

Typical elements

- Technology
- Competition
- Economy
- Regulation
- Society
- Geopolitics
- Customer behaviour

Characteristics

- Continuous change
- Episodic disruption
- Opportunities
- Threats
- Uncertainty

Represented by

Terrain / topographic map.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Purpose
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The objectives the enterprise exists to achieve.

Purpose

Defines what success looks like.

Examples

- Growth
- Profitability
- Innovation
- Cost leadership
- Quality
- Sustainability
- Public value
- Market leadership

Relationship

Purpose together with the Operating Landscape determines what enterprise should be built.

------------------------------------------------------------
Capability
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

A coherent organisational ability required to create, govern, deliver or support value.

Purpose

Capabilities are the fundamental building blocks of Enterprise Dynamics.

Examples

- Commercial
- Engineering
- Delivery
- Governance
- Finance
- Innovation

What it is not

- Department
- Team
- Job title
- Reporting line

------------------------------------------------------------
Capability Domain
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

A logical grouping of related enterprise capabilities.

Reference domains

- Strategy
- Commercial
- Product / Service
- Innovation
- Engineering / Build
- Delivery
- Operations
- Enterprise Services

------------------------------------------------------------
Capability Architecture
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The composition, interaction and evolution of enterprise capabilities.

Purpose

Represents what the enterprise is built from.

Capabilities may emerge, grow, shrink, split, merge and retire.

Represented by interlocking gears.

Does NOT represent organisation charts, reporting lines or headcount.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Motion
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The evolution of enterprise capabilities through time.

Purpose

Explains how enterprises continuously evolve.

Relationship

Enterprise Motion is governed by the Enterprise Clock.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Clock
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The mechanism that synchronises the evolution of the Capability Architecture.

Purpose

Ensures capabilities evolve coherently despite operating at different natural cadences.

Represents

- Synchronisation
- Coupled evolution
- Time

Does NOT represent project schedules or deadlines.

------------------------------------------------------------
Natural Cadence
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The inherent rate at which a capability should evolve according to its purpose.

Principle

Capabilities should evolve at their natural cadence rather than identical speeds.

------------------------------------------------------------
Synchronisation
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The coordinated evolution of enterprise capabilities over time.

Purpose

Ensures capability evolution remains coherent.

------------------------------------------------------------
Dynamic Equilibrium
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The continual balancing of enterprise capabilities against changing operating conditions.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Direction
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The deliberate guidance of enterprise evolution.

Represented by the Steering Wheel and Enterprise Gyroscope.

------------------------------------------------------------
Steering Wheel
------------------------------------------------------------

Represents deliberate leadership intervention through strategy, governance, capital allocation, incentives and organisation design.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Gyroscope
------------------------------------------------------------

Represents mechanisms preserving enterprise coherence during change through governance, decision principles, risk appetite, culture and institutional memory.

------------------------------------------------------------
Stable Orientation
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The preservation of coherent enterprise direction throughout continuous evolution.

------------------------------------------------------------
Institutionalisation
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

Embedding judgement, authority and decision logic within the enterprise rather than individual leaders.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Resilience
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The emergent ability of an enterprise to remain fit for purpose while continuously adapting.

------------------------------------------------------------
Fit for Purpose
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The degree to which an enterprise remains appropriate for its objectives and operating landscape.

------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise Evolution
------------------------------------------------------------

Definition

The continuous adaptation of capability architecture, enterprise motion and enterprise direction over time.
