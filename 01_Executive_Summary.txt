Enterprise Dynamics - Executive Summary

Enterprise Dynamics is a systems theory explaining how enterprises
continuously evolve to remain fit for purpose as their operating
landscape, objectives and available capabilities change over time.

The theory proposes that enterprises should be viewed as dynamic systems
rather than static organisational structures. Leaders must continuously
adapt capability architecture while maintaining synchronised evolution
and coherent strategic direction.

The theory is organised around: - Operating Landscape - Enterprise
Purpose - Capability Architecture - Enterprise Motion - Enterprise
Direction - Enterprise Resilience
