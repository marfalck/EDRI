Definitions

Operating Landscape: External conditions within which the enterprise
operates.

Enterprise Purpose: Desired outcomes the enterprise seeks to achieve.

Capability: Organisational ability required to create, govern, deliver
or support value.

Capability Architecture: Composition and interaction of enterprise
capabilities.

Enterprise Clock: Mechanism synchronising capability evolution over
time.

Steering Wheel: Leadership intervention.

Enterprise Gyroscope: Mechanism preserving strategic coherence.

Enterprise Resilience: Emergent property resulting from sustained fit
for purpose.
